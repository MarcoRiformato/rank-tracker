📖 Understanding Competitor Metrics

Dominance Score (0-3.0)
Measures how well a competitor ranks across all locations. Formula: (Top 3 appearances × 3 + Top 10 appearances × 2 + Top 30 appearances × 1) ÷ Total appearances. Higher = better average positioning.
Consistency
Standard deviation of ranks. Lower values mean more stable/consistent rankings across locations. A consistency of 2.0 means ranks typically vary by ±2 positions.
Growth Trend
Compares recent rankings vs older rankings. ↑ Improving = ranks getting better, ↓ Declining = ranks worsening, → Stable = little change.
Classification Types
Falling Giant High dominance but declining trend
Growing Giant High dominance + improving trend
Emerging Low dominance but improving + recent appearance
Stable Leader High dominance + stable + consistent top positions
Coverage %
Percentage of scanned locations where this competitor appears at least once in the top 30 results.
Average Rank
Mean ranking position when the competitor appears. Lower = better (1 is best, 30 is worst).
